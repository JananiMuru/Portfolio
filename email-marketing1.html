Case Studies

Case Study 01

Email List Revival & Cold Audience Optimization

Revived a cold, low-performing email list through data-driven cleaning, segmentation & targeted re-engagement.

01

Objective

Revive a low-performing email list and improve engagement.

02

Problem

~20% bounce rate, low open & click rates, cold unengaged audience.

03

Strategy

List cleaning + segmentation, re-engagement campaigns, cold email optimization.

04

Execution

Removed inactive/invalid contacts, built re-engagement campaigns, improved subject lines.

05

Results

Bounce rate: 20% → 0.01% · Open rates ↑ ~20% · CTR ↑ ~5%

★

Key Takeaway

Consistent list hygiene, smart segmentation and re-engagement keep the list healthy and drive sustainable performance.
