Creative Exploration

These projects reflect my curiosity, and approach to exploring ideas beyond structured marketing work.

01

Podcast Editing

Edited podcast episodes as part of my internship, focusing on creating a smooth and engaging listening experience. From refining audio clarity to shaping the overall flow and pacing, this work helped me understand storytelling from an auditory perspective—where timing, tone, and structure play a key role in keeping listeners engaged.

Audio Editing Storytelling Content Production

02

Newspaper Publication Design

Worked as a Graphic Designer for The Washtenaw Voice, an award-winning student newspaper publication. Collaborated with a team of writers, editors, and designers to create clean, structured layouts that balanced readability with visual appeal. This experience strengthened my ability to design within real-world editorial constraints while maintaining consistency across issues.

Editorial Design Layout Design Print Media

03

Recipe Booklet Design

Designed a compact recipe booklet for Green Things Farm Collective, a contemporary farm business based in Ann Arbor. The challenge was to create an informative and visually engaging booklet using a single A4 sheet and black ink. By designing a double-sided layout that folds into an eight-page format, the final piece was both functional and easy to package with products, balancing practicality with thoughtful design.

Editorial Design Print Design Layout Systems

04

Long Format Video Editing

Edited long-form video content for YouTube as part of my internship, focusing on pacing, storytelling, and viewer retention. This involved structuring content to maintain engagement over longer durations, refining transitions, and ensuring a cohesive narrative flow from start to finish.
