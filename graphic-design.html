Project 01

Sweet Afton Brand Design

Reimagined the brand identity for Sweet Afton, a tearoom based in Plymouth, Michigan. The logo features a custom monogram where the letterforms subtly represent a teacup and steam, reinforcing the brand's identity in a simple, memorable way. The overall design balances warmth and sophistication, making the brand feel both inviting and contemporary.

Project 02

Soothe Brand Design

Developed a complete visual identity for Soothe, a fictional healthcare brand designed for children under the age of 13. A custom hand-lettered logo and soft, rounded typography were used to convey warmth and approachability. Primary colors were intentionally chosen to create familiarity and build trust, while maintaining a playful tone that resonates with children and parents alike.

Project 03

Typographics Event Design

Created a cohesive brand identity for Typographics, a design festival centered around contemporary typography. The concept was built around geometric simplicity, using basic shapes and structured layouts to reflect the precision and versatility of typography. The final output included a consistent identity across event materials, balancing creativity with clarity.

Project 04

Magazine Design

Designed a print-focused magazine exploring the world of print production, its processes, and the people behind it. The layout emphasizes readability, structure, and visual rhythm, drawing inspiration from editorial design systems. Careful attention was given to typography, spacing, and hierarchy to create a clean and immersive reading experience. The overall design reflects a balance between informative content and strong visual storytelling.
